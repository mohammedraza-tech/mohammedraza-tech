## 🧭 About Me

I'm a full-stack developer and AI automation builder who works across the entire
stack rather than staying narrow to one layer — frontend, backend, infrastructure,
or automation, depending on what a project actually needs.

I lean heavily on AI-assisted development to move fast without cutting corners on
quality — the goal is always a working, polished result, not just quick output.
I care about the details most people skip: motion, interaction, clean structure,
and code that's actually maintainable, not just functional.

Currently taking on freelance projects. Early clients get full focus, fast
turnaround, and a fair price while I build out a track record of shipped work.

<br>

## 🛠️ What I Build

**Websites & MVPs**
Landing pages, portfolio sites, and first working versions of a product —
built and shipped quickly rather than over-engineered from day one.

**Internal Tools & Dashboards**
Simple, purpose-built tools for tracking, managing, or visualizing data —
without the bloat of software you don't actually need.

**AI Automation & Agents**
Scripts, integrations, and AI-driven workflows that remove repetitive work —
data entry, reporting, content generation, API pipelines, and agent-based
automation.

**Scroll-Driven & Interactive Experiences**
Motion-first interfaces using WebGL/3D and scroll-synced animation, for
projects where the experience itself needs to feel considered, not templated.
